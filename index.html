Why we started

Honesty over hype

Most dry fruit sold in India today sits on supermarket shelves for months — sometimes years — slowly losing its crunch, flavour and nutrients. Add to that the confusing labelling, the artificial brightening, the polished shells, and the sometimes eye-watering markups, and it becomes hard to know what you're really buying.

We started Blora Foods to do the opposite. We buy direct from growers and trusted wholesalers in small batches, pack fresh every week in our Kancheepuram facility, and ship straight to your door. No middlemen, no months-old stock, no artificial polish — just clean, traceable dry fruits at a price that makes sense.

Every order is packed by hand, weighed on a calibrated scale, and sealed in food-grade resealable pouches. We're FSSAI certified, and we're proud that a growing number of Kancheepuram and Chennai households treat Blora as their regular dry-fruits supplier — not just for festivals.

From farm to your door

How a Blora pack is made

01

Source from trusted growers

Cashews from the Konkan coast, walnuts from Kashmir, almonds from California, raisins from Theni. We work with a small list of trusted growers we've personally vetted.

02

Inspect & grade in Kancheepuram

Every batch is opened, inspected for size and freshness, and graded by hand. Broken, discoloured or stale pieces are removed.

03

Pack in food-grade pouches

We weigh on calibrated scales and seal in resealable, food-grade pouches with clear labels — variety, weight, pack date, FSSAI details.

04

Ship within 24 hours

Orders placed before 4pm ship the same day via tracked courier. Local Kancheepuram orders are hand-delivered the same evening.
